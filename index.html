Featured work | Business Operations - Case Management & Reporting

Accounts Receivable Ageing

Established a clear case-ownership and escalation workflow, connecting stakeholders with internal teams to resolve overdue cases.

At a glance

Company / context
SRI Pte Ltd, Singapore
Role
Systems Analyst - workflow design and development
Skills
Cross-functional stakeholder coordination, Case ownership, Escalation management, Workflow optimization, Reporting automation
Collaborators
Salespersons, Finance team, Compliance team, and external stakeholders

The problem

Without a centralized workflow, overdue cases lacked clear ownership and progress visibility, making follow-ups and escalations difficult. Operational reporting also relied on time-consuming manual work.

My approach

I analyzed the existing processes, identified pain points, and collaborated with stakeholders to define requirements to design a solution that streamlined workflows and improved data visibility.

The solution

I built an integrated Excel & Notion tracking workflow that brought together case status, ownership, and reporting into one shared process.

Impact

Reduced the overdue-case backlog by 50% for a population of 2,000+ agents. Centralized reporting in Excel and Notion, cutting report preparation time by 70%.

What I learned

Enhanced my understanding of systems design and the importance of user-centered approaches in creating effective solutions.

A centralized hub that made essential design and company information easier to find.

Role
Project Driver, UI/UX Designer, Website Developer
Skills
Research, information architecture, systems design, web development, UAT

Featured work | Design Operations - Workflow Automation

DSGN Library Management System

An automated borrowing workflow for tracking books, requests, and return reminders.

At a glance

Company / context
Traveloka design team
Role
Project Manager, System Architect
Skills
Process design, automation, data management, stakeholder coordination
Collaborators
VP of Design and Traveloka employees

The problem

The previous lending tool was difficult to access and lacked a structured return schedule, leaving a lot of physical books untracked and at risk of being lost or damaged.

My approach

I mapped the borrowing flow with the VP of Design and selected tools already available across the company to reduce access barriers.

The solution

I built a borrowing workflow in which borrowers submit requests through Google Forms, Google Sheets tracks borrowing status, and Google Apps Script creates return reminders in borrowers’ Google Calendars.

Impact

The workflow introduced consistent return reminders and a shared record of borrowing activity, giving the team a more reliable way to manage the library and protect company assets.

What I learned

I learned that technology is a powerful tool for solving complex problems, but it must be implemented thoughtfully to ensure it meets user needs and integrates seamlessly with existing processes.

Digital Experience - Campaign Design

Payday promotion experience

A redesigned payday page organized promotions from six products across domestic and international travel.

At a glance

Company / context
Traveloka Indonesia
Role
UI/UX Designer, Website Developer, Design QA
Skills
Interaction design, content structure, web delivery
Collaborators
Product and Marketing teams

The problem

One recurring page needed to accommodate promotions from six products across domestic and international travel.

My approach

I tested an initial table-like structure, observed misleading click behavior, and used those findings to reorganize the page.

The solution

I iteratively placed product labels above campaign thumbnails and separated domestic and international promotions into two clear columns.

Impact

The revised layout remained in use throughout the promotion plan for over a year.
