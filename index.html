Payday promotion experience

A revised campaign structure that helped users find relevant promotions with less confusion.

At a glance

Company / context
Traveloka Indonesia
Timeline
Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet.
Role
UI/UX Designer, Website Developer, Design QA
Skills
Interaction design, content structure, web delivery, QA
Collaborators
Product and campaign stakeholders

The problem

One recurring page needed to accommodate promotions from six products across domestic and international travel.

My approach

I tested an initial table-like structure, observed misleading click behavior, and used those findings to reorganize the page.

The solution

The iteration placed product labels above campaign thumbnails and separated domestic and international promotions into two clear columns.

Impact

The revised page remained in use through the promotion plan. Add click-through, conversion, or sales data here.

What I learned

The location and affordance of a label can change user behavior; clickable and non-clickable elements need unmistakable hierarchy.
